From IT operations to security consulting.

I'm a Cybersecurity Analytics and Operations student at Penn State, with graduation expected in December 2026. My focus is offensive security: understanding how systems work, testing where they fail, and explaining what needs to change.

During my summer 2026 internship as a Security Consultant at SEVN-X, I performed internal, external, and web application penetration tests. I also supported a ransomware preparedness assessment, interviewing client security teams, mapping findings to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and contributing remediation guidance to final reports. That work reinforced the importance of connecting technical findings to decisions a client can act on.

Earlier, at Excelligence Learning Corporation, I administered Active Directory onboarding and offboarding, automated onboarding with PowerShell and Windows image deployment, and assessed Windows 11 systems against CIS Benchmarks. That operational foundation informs the way I approach security testing.

Build, compete, and teach.

Through CCSO, I conduct client penetration tests and vulnerability assessments, contribute findings and remediation guidance, and participate in red team operations against CCDC competitor blue teams and in internal exercises.

I've developed a deliberately misconfigured cyber range for Red vs. Blue exercises, using Ansible to deploy infrastructure and seeded vulnerabilities. I also lead workshops on web application testing, Active Directory exploitation, networking, and Bash and PowerShell scripting.

Teaching low-level concepts has made me more deliberate about explaining the reasoning behind a technique.
